TrabahoLens — Philippine Job Market Visualizer

Explore Philippine labour statistics and model-estimated AI task exposure. Choose a layer, then select a box to inspect its evidence. Area and colour have different meanings across layers; see Method & limitations.

Caveat: Task exposure is a model estimate and has not been independently validated. It is not an estimate of automation, displacement, layoffs, or future employment. Unavailable means unavailable—not zero.

Method & limitations A guide to the map

Start with the map

Choose a layer, then hover for a quick preview or select a box to explore its evidence.

Colour
The value in the selected layer. On task exposure, green means lower exposure and red means higher exposure.
Box size
Employed persons on survey layers. Exposure uses the cube of listed PSA task counts, a display stretch—not worker counts. Wage references use equal boxes.

Understand the score

Explore each point on the task-exposure scale. These are ordered categories, not percentages.

Keep the limits in view

Generative-AI task exposure indicates how much an occupation's tasks may be affected or assisted by generative AI. It is not an estimate of automation, displacement, layoffs, or future employment.

No independent Philippine statistical review has been completed. Missing values stay missing.
