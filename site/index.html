TrabahoLens — Philippine Job Market Visualizer

Explore Philippine occupations through PSA sources. In AI Task Exposure, each box is one PSOC occupation and its colour shows how much of the tasks PSA lists for it an AI model judged generative AI could assist with or perform. Box size is equal for every occupation; it does not represent employment.

Other layers: Employment sizes PSA major occupation groups by employed persons. Wage references and OFW distribution describe separate survey populations and are not interchangeable. Select any box for its source, tasks and limitations.

Caveat: Task exposure is experimental and not yet human-reviewed. It is not an estimate of automation, displacement, layoffs, or future employment. Unavailable means unavailable—not zero.

Method & limitations

Generative-AI task exposure indicates how much an occupation's tasks may be affected or assisted by generative AI. It is not an estimate of automation, displacement, layoffs, or future employment.
