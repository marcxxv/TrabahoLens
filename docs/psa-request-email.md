**Subject:** Request for occupation-level labour statistics for a public dashboard (TrabahoLens)

Dear Philippine Statistics Authority,

I am building TrabahoLens, an open dashboard of the Philippine job market that is
planned to be featured by the Philippine AI Report. It uses only official
published figures and shows the source, table, reference period and precision
beside every number. I would like to ask for the following. Where something
cannot be released reliably, please say so. I would rather show a gap than a
weak estimate.

1. **Employed persons by detailed occupation.** Annual estimates for 2024 and the
   latest comparable year, at four-digit PSOC (2012, with the 2022 updates)
   if possible, otherwise three-digit. Please include the PSOC version, weighted
   national estimates, the estimation method, standard errors or coefficients of
   variation, suppression rules, and a stable URL or source file.
2. **Pay by occupation.** Average basic pay (daily or monthly) of wage and salary
   workers by major occupation group or finer, from the Labor Force Survey or
   another PSA source. We found LFS pay only by industry, and Occupational Wages
   Survey pay only for benchmark positions.
3. **Educational attainment by occupation.** Employed persons by highest grade
   completed within each major occupation group or finer.
4. **Informal employment by occupation.** The informal-sector or informal-employment
   share within each major occupation group, with the definition used.
5. **Vacancies or job-posting demand by occupation.** Anything newer than the
   2021-2022 ISLE occupational shortages tables (for example 1B3ES031).
6. **Reuse.** Please confirm the terms for republishing these figures, with
   attribution, on a public website.

If any of this exists as a published table I have missed, a link is enough.
Please also tell me if a fee or a formal letter to the National Statistician is
required.

Purpose: public, non-commercial information on the Philippine labour market.
