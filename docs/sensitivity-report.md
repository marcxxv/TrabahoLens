## What this report will contain once a run exists

| Analysis | Method | Release gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Within-model stability | Range and standard deviation across replicates of the same model and prompt | A task whose replicates span more than 1 point is reported unstable; an occupation where more than 25% of tasks are unstable is not published as a point score |
| Between-model agreement | Mean absolute difference and exact-agreement share across models | Reported; large disagreement is published rather than resolved by choosing a favourite |
| Prompt-wording sensitivity | Re-run under a variant prompt at the same rubric version | A dimension that moves materially on paraphrase is reported as prompt-sensitive |
| Task-weighting sensitivity | Unweighted vs importance-weighted aggregation | Both published; never averaged together |
| Crosswalk-quality sensitivity | Mean score by crosswalk match quality | A systematic gradient means low-quality mappings are suppressed |
| Comparison with an established methodology | Against a published task-exposure study where licensing permits | Reported as concordance, not as validation |
| Face validity | Manual review of the watchlist below | Reviewer sign-off recorded in `human_review_status` |

These groups are where a task-based measure is most likely to mislead, so

**Philippine specific concern**

- 91 Cleaners and Helpers (kasambahay, domestic work)
- 95 Street and Related Sales and Service Workers (vendors)
- 83 Drivers and Mobile Plant Operators (tricycle, jeepney, delivery riders)
- 62 Market-oriented Skilled Forestry, Fishery and Hunting Workers (fisherfolk)
- 35 Information and Communications Technicians (BPO and contact-centre work)

Specific failure modes to look for:

- **Physical and embodied work scored above 1 on dimension A.** Usually a sign
  the model scored the occupation's documentation rather than its work.
- **High-touch service scored high on A and low on B.** The two are
  independent; a care role that scores low on B has been misread.
- **Mixed occupations collapsing to their digital half.** A nurse's charting
  is not a nurse's job; check the task list is not documentation-heavy.
- **Philippine-specific work scored from a US task profile.** Kasambahay,
  tricycle and jeepney driving, street vending and fisherfolk have no close
  O\*NET analogue; a confident score here is a crosswalk failure, and
  dimension D should be low.
- **BPO and contact-centre work.** The occupation most exposed to this
  measure's headline framing in the Philippines, and the one where the gap
  between task exposure and employment outcome is widest. Treat any
  reporting of it with particular care.
